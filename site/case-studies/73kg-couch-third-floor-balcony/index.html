How We Lifted a 73kg Couch to a Third-Floor Balcony

Based on a published customer review describing a third-floor balcony lift for a 73kg couch in Sydney.

This job came from a familiar Sydney access problem: a long-awaited lounge arrived, but the couch would not fit through the front door. The customer needed a practical solution to get a 73kg couch into a third-floor apartment without damaging the item or the property.

The Problem

The item was a 73kg couch that could not be moved through the normal entry point. The front door was not a viable path, which meant the usual carry-in approach had already failed.

The Assessment

The key decision was whether the couch could be brought in safely via the balcony instead of through the internal entry route. This is exactly the kind of situation where a balcony lift becomes the practical option.

The Job

BG Delivery lifted the couch over the third-floor balcony and got it into place. According to the live review, the team made the job look easy even though the original delivery path had already failed.

The Result

The couch reached the apartment without needing to be exchanged or abandoned because of access. The customer described the team as considerate and said they would use BG Delivery again in the future.
