Meet BG Delivery

BG Delivery helps Sydney customers solve the kinds of moving problems that standard removalists often avoid: oversized furniture, tight stairwells, difficult apartment access, balcony lifts, bulky King Living pieces, and smaller moves where careful handling matters.

A Sydney Team Built Around Difficult Access Jobs

Many customers contact BG Delivery after discovering that their couch will not fit the lift, their furniture cannot clear a narrow stairwell, or a bulky item needs to reach a balcony apartment safely. That kind of work requires more than basic transport. It requires planning, careful handling, and a team that stays calm when a job is awkward, heavy, or time-sensitive.

What Customers Usually Call Us For

  • balcony lifts for couches, sofas, beds, and oversized furniture
  • King Living furniture moves and difficult access handling
  • small removals where one or two bulky items are the real challenge
  • apartment moves involving stair, lift, or access constraints
  • difficult access jobs that other crews treat as too hard
