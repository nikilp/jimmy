Balcony Lift and Small Removals FAQ

These answers cover the most common quote, access, apartment, and furniture-lifting questions that come up before a job is booked.

What to expect

Most jobs can be assessed much faster once the team has photos of the item, building access, and any likely problem points such as stairs, lifts, or balconies.

What details should I send for a quote?

Send photos of the item, the building entrance, any stairwells or lifts involved, the balcony or access point if relevant, the suburb, and the floor level.

How do I know if I need a balcony lift?

If the furniture will not fit safely through the front door, hallway, stairwell, or building lift, a balcony lift may be the best option.

Do you only handle large moves?

No. BG Delivery also handles small removals, limited-item jobs, and urgent furniture moves where access or bulky items are the main issue.

Do you work with apartment buildings?

Yes. Apartment access issues are one of the main reasons customers contact BG Delivery, especially for oversized couches, beds, and bulky furniture.

Can you help with King Living furniture?

Yes. King Living furniture is one of the recurring job types reflected in the reviews and on the dedicated service page.

What if other removalists say the job cannot be done?

That is often when customers contact BG Delivery. The next step is a proper access review based on photos and dimensions rather than a guess.
